# Globex Corporation — Information Handling & Procurement Policy
Policy ref: GP-SEC-2026-2 · Owner: Chief Information Security Officer · Version 3.1 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Review due: 31 December 2026

---PAGE---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the minimum requirements for handling company information and for procuring third-party services at Globex Corporation. It applies to all employees, contractors and interns ("staff") and to all company systems, whether on-premises or in the cloud. Line managers are accountable for compliance within their teams.

This policy does not replace local statutory requirements; where local law is stricter, local law prevails.

---PAGE---

## 2. Information classification

All information must be classified at creation into one of four levels:

- **Public** — approved for publication. No handling restrictions.
- **Internal** — for staff use only. Stored in company-managed systems; sharing outside Globex requires manager approval.
- **Confidential** — commercially sensitive. Encrypted at rest and in transit; access on a need-to-know basis; sharing with third parties requires a signed NDA and a data processing agreement where personal data is involved.
- **Restricted** — regulated or critical personal data. Requires named individual access, logging of every access, and Chief Information Security Officer approval for any third-party processing.

Unclassified information is treated as Internal by default.

---PAGE---

## 3. Procurement of third-party services

Any service that stores, processes or can access Confidential or Restricted information requires a security review before signature. The review covers data residency, encryption, sub-processors, breach notification timelines and the right to audit. Suppliers holding Confidential data must sign the standard Data Processing Agreement, provide ISO 27001 certification or an equivalent attestation, and notify Globex of confirmed security incidents within 24 hours.

Contracts above USD 100,000 total value additionally require Chief Financial Officer approval and a competitive tender with at least two written proposals. Procurement must document the selected supplier, the decision rationale and the rejected alternatives in the vendor register.

---PAGE---

## 4. Payment and expense rules

Supplier invoices are matched to purchase orders by Finance before payment. Standard payment terms are net 30; deviation beyond net 45 requires Finance Director approval and must be recorded in the contract. Early-payment discounts above 3% may be accepted without additional approval.

Staff expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims above USD 500 require receipts and line-manager approval; above USD 2,000, Finance Director approval. Personal use of company payment cards is prohibited and is treated as gross misconduct.

---PAGE---

## 5. Violations and enforcement

Breaches of this policy are handled under the disciplinary procedure and may lead to termination. Suspected data loss must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour of discovery; failure to report is itself a violation. Knowingly classifying information at a lower level to avoid controls is treated as a serious breach.

Exceptions to any control in this policy require written approval from the policy owner, a documented compensating control, and a maximum validity of 12 months.
