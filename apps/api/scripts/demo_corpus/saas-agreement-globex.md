# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
## Between Globex Corporation ("Customer") and Acme Cloud Solutions Ltd. ("Supplier")
Agreement no. GLX-MSA-2026-0089 · Effective date: 1 April 2026

---PAGE---

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

"Services" means the subscription services, implementation services and support services described in Statement of Work SOW-2026-14 and its successors. "Documentation" means the user guides and API references made available with the Services. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by a party, whether marked confidential or not. "Personal Data" has the meaning given in applicable data protection law.

Headings are for convenience only. References to days mean calendar days unless stated as business days.

---PAGE---

## 2. Term and termination

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 24 months. It renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

Either party may terminate for material breach that remains uncured 30 days after written notice. Customer may terminate for convenience on 60 days' written notice, in which case the Supplier refunds the unused portion of prepaid subscription fees on a pro-rata basis, less the 5% second-year discount already granted if the termination occurs within the first commitment year.

---PAGE---

## 3. Fees, invoicing and payment

Customer shall pay the fees set out in the Order Form: a subscription fee of USD 8,000 per month, an implementation fee of USD 34,500, and a support fee of USD 1,500 per month. Invoices are issued monthly in arrears and are payable net 30 from the invoice date.

Late payments bear interest at 1.0% per month, compounded, and the Supplier may suspend the Services if any amount remains unpaid more than 15 days after a second written reminder. Disputed invoices must be raised in good faith within 10 business days of receipt; undisputed amounts remain payable. All fees are exclusive of taxes.

---PAGE---

## 4. Service levels and support

The Supplier shall provide the Services with monthly uptime of at least 99.9%, measured excluding scheduled maintenance announced 5 business days in advance. Severity 1 incidents receive a response within 1 hour, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Severity 2 incidents receive a response within 4 business hours.

If the Supplier misses the uptime target in any month, Customer receives a service credit of 10% of the monthly subscription fee, capped at two consecutive months of credits per incident root cause. Service credits are the exclusive remedy for availability failures.

---PAGE---

## 5. Data protection and security

The Supplier processes Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer and acts as processor. All Customer data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt region) and is encrypted in transit and at rest. The Supplier maintains ISO 27001 certification and SOC 2 Type II reports, provides annual summaries on request, and notifies Customer of any personal data breach within 24 hours of becoming aware.

On termination, the Supplier exports Customer data in standard formats within 30 days and deletes remaining copies within 90 days, except where retention is required by law.

---PAGE---

## 6. Liability and indemnities

Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement is capped at the total fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for lost profits or lost savings.

The Supplier shall defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Services infringe intellectual property rights, provided Customer notifies the Supplier promptly and grants control of the defence. The Supplier may replace or modify infringing services. If neither is commercially practicable, Customer may terminate and receive a pro-rata refund.

Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 7. Governing law and general provisions

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction. Neither party may assign the Agreement without consent, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect. The parties are independent contractors; nothing creates a partnership or agency. This Agreement, with its Order Forms and SOWs, is the entire agreement and supersedes all prior proposals and understandings, including any pre-contractual descriptions of features not documented in the Documentation.
