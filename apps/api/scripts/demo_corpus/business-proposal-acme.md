# Business Proposal — Enterprise Document Platform
## Acme Cloud Solutions Ltd.
Proposal reference: ACS-2026-114 · Prepared for: Globex Corporation · Date: 12 February 2026

---PAGE---

## 1. Executive summary

Acme Cloud Solutions Ltd. ("Acme") proposes to supply Globex Corporation ("Globex") with its enterprise document intelligence platform, including implementation, data migration and premium support. The total contract value is USD 148,500 for an initial 12-month term. Acme has delivered equivalent programmes for 34 enterprise customers since 2021, with a median go-live of 9 weeks.

This proposal responds to Globex's RFP RG-2026-03 issued 20 January 2026. It remains valid for 60 days from the date above.

---PAGE---

## 2. Scope of services

The engagement covers three workstreams:

1. **Platform licensing** — 850 named users on the Acme Enterprise tier, with unlimited documents and API access at 50 requests per second.
2. **Implementation** — discovery workshop (2 weeks), configuration and single-sign-on integration (4 weeks), data migration of up to 250,000 legacy documents (3 weeks), and user acceptance testing (2 weeks).
3. **Premium support** — 24/7 support with a 1-hour response target for Severity 1 incidents and a named customer success manager.

Out of scope: hardware procurement, custom machine-learning model development, and translation services. These can be added by change order.

## 3. Commercials and payment terms

The total price of USD 148,500 is composed of:

- Platform licensing: USD 96,000 per year (USD 8,000 per month)
- Implementation services: USD 34,500 one-off
- Premium support: USD 18,000 per year

**Payment schedule.** Invoices are issued monthly in arrears. Payment terms are net 30 from invoice date. The one-off implementation fee is invoiced in two equal instalments: 50% at contract signature and 50% at go-live acceptance. Late payments accrue interest at 1.0% per month. All prices are exclusive of VAT and any withholding taxes, which Globex shall pay in addition.

A 5% discount on the second year applies if Globex commits to a 24-month term at signature.

## 5. Risk considerations

Acme identifies the following delivery risks for joint management:

- **Migration scope risk** — legacy documents of poor scan quality may require re-processing; the 250,000-document allowance includes a 15% contingency.
- **Dependency risk** — the single-sign-on integration requires Globex identity-provider changes that have historically taken 4–6 weeks in comparable engagements.
- **Key-person risk** — the migration specialist role is single-headed; Acme will cross-train a second engineer during week 4.

Data processed under this engagement remains the property of Globex. Acme acts as processor under the Globex Data Processing Agreement and stores all data in the EU (Frankfurt region), with ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II attestations available on request.
