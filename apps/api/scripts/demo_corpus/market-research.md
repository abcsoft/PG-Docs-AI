# Market Research — European Document Intelligence Software, 2026
## Globex Strategy Office · Internal report MR-2026-07 · February 2026

---PAGE---

## 1. Market size and growth

The European document intelligence software market — covering capture, classification, extraction and retrieval with AI assistance — was worth an estimated EUR 2.9 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach EUR 5.6 billion by 2030, a compound annual growth rate of 14.1%. Growth is concentrated in financial services, insurance, legal services and the public sector, which together account for 61% of 2025 spending.

The fastest-growing segment is AI-assisted contract analysis, which grew 27% year over year as legal teams reduced external counsel spend. The slowest segment is traditional OCR capture, growing at 3% as buyers consolidate vendors.

## 2. Buyer priorities

Our survey of 214 procurement and IT decision-makers across the EU, run in December 2025, ranks buying criteria as follows:

1. Data residency and compliance (rated critical by 78% of respondents) — EU storage is a hard requirement for 54%.
2. Answer traceability — 71% require citations linking AI answers to source passages before deployment.
3. Total cost of ownership — median acceptable spend is EUR 150 per user per year for enterprises above 5,000 staff.
4. Integration with existing repositories — SharePoint and Salesforce adapters are requested by 63%.
5. Model transparency — 44% ask for the right to audit prompts and outputs.

Only 12% of respondents prioritised on-premises deployment, down from 31% in 2023.

## 3. Competitive landscape

The market splits into three groups:

- **Suite vendors** (46% combined share) — broad enterprise-content platforms adding AI features; strong on integration, weaker on answer quality.
- **AI-native specialists** (38% share, growing fastest at 21% per year) — built around retrieval and generation with citations; typically win on traceability and time-to-value.
- **Managed service providers** (16% share) — operate the whole pipeline as a service; attractive to mid-market buyers without ML teams, at a 20-30% price premium per processed document.

Pricing benchmarks for a 1,000-user enterprise: suite vendors EUR 110-160 per user per year; specialists EUR 90-140; managed services EUR 0.08-0.15 per document plus a platform fee.

## 4. Risks and market watch-items

- **Regulatory** — the EU AI Act transparency obligations are expected to apply to document-AI deployments from August 2026; 68% of surveyed buyers have not yet assessed readiness.
- **Vendor consolidation** — three AI-native specialists were acquired in 2025; contract terms should protect against product retirement.
- **Cost drift** — usage-based pricing without caps produced bill shocks for 22% of adopters in our sample; buyers increasingly demand monthly spend ceilings.
- **Quality claims** — vendor-stated extraction accuracy averaged 96% in marketing material versus 89% measured in our reference checks.
